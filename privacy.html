Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Who we are

Meridian Dental Studio ("we", "us", "our") operates this website and the dental clinic located at 218 Rio Grande Street, Suite 400, Austin, TX 78701. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us. When you request an appointment, contact us, or join our membership plan, we collect the details you provide — typically your name, phone number, email address, preferred appointment times, and any message you choose to send.

Health information. Information relating to your dental care is collected and handled under our HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices, which is provided at your first visit and available on request from our front desk.

Basic technical data. Like most websites, our hosting provider may log standard technical information (browser type, pages visited, approximate region) for security and performance purposes.

3. How we use your information

We use the information you provide to schedule and confirm appointments, respond to your questions, prepare treatment estimates, process insurance claims, and — only with your consent — send occasional reminders or practice updates. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their marketing.

4. Appointment requests through this website

Submitting the appointment form sends your request to our front-desk team, who use it solely to contact you about booking. Please note that standard email and web forms are not fully secure; for anything sensitive, call us at (512) 555-0184. Do not include detailed medical history in the form message field.

5. Third-party services

We use a small number of trusted providers to operate this site and clinic — for example, our map provider (OpenStreetMap) and our SMS/email messaging service. These providers process data only on our instructions. Embedded content (such as the map) may be loaded from the provider's servers when you view it.

6. Cookies

This website uses only essential, privacy-respecting functionality and does not set advertising or tracking cookies. If that ever changes, we will update this policy and provide an appropriate consent notice.

7. Data retention & security

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above and as required by healthcare record-retention law. We apply reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to a dental practice; no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, subject to healthcare record-keeping obligations. You may also withdraw consent for non-essential communications at any time. To exercise any of these rights, email hello@meridiandental.com or call (512) 555-0184.

9. Children's privacy

We provide dental care to children, but this website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children through the website. Requests for pediatric appointments should be made by a parent or guardian.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above reflects the current version. Material changes will be highlighted on this page.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy: hello@meridiandental.com · (512) 555-0184 · 218 Rio Grande Street, Suite 400, Austin, TX 78701.
