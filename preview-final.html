About the Clinic

Dentistry designed around you.

Meridian was founded on a simple belief: great dentistry should feel calm, honest, and personal — never rushed, never salesy.

We combine specialty-level expertise with technology that makes treatment faster, gentler, and more predictable. Every plan starts with listening: your history, your concerns, and the smile you actually want — then we design the shortest, most comfortable path to it.

Yes. We hold same-day emergency slots every weekday. If you call before 3pm with pain, swelling, or a broken tooth, we will almost always see you that day. After hours, our voicemail directs you to the on-call dentist.

Every plan begins with a written, itemized estimate before any treatment starts — no exceptions. As a guide: new-patient consultations are $95, hygiene visits start at $140, and whitening from $399. Complex treatment is always quoted in full, with options at different price points.

Yes. Treatment over $500 can be spread across interest-free monthly installments, and we offer an in-house membership plan ($29/month) that covers checkups, hygiene, X-rays, and discounts on further treatment for uninsured patients.

It depends on the treatment: whitening is a single visit, most implants complete in 3–4 months, clear aligners typically run 6–15 months, and veneers take two visits about two weeks apart. Your written plan will include a realistic timeline before you commit.

We accept most major PPO plans and file claims on your behalf. Bring your insurance card to your first visit and we'll verify your benefits before treatment — you'll always know your expected out-of-pocket cost up front.
